## How Does It Work?

Telegram uses a link protocol called `t.me` that lets anyone open a chat directly. When you visit `t.me/username` in a browser, it launches a Telegram conversation with that user — no contact saving required. For phone numbers, the format is `t.me/+phonenumber`.

OPN Chat simplifies this process. Instead of manually constructing URLs, you:

1. **Enter a Telegram @username** (for example, @durov) **or a phone number** with the country code.
2. **Optionally write a message** that will be pre-filled in the chat.
3. **Click "Open Chat in Telegram"** — and you are taken directly to the conversation.

That is it. No sign-up, no app to install beyond Telegram itself, no data stored on any server. Everything runs inside your browser.

## Username vs. Phone Number — Which Should You Use?

OPN Chat supports both methods, and each has its advantages:

**Using a @username** is ideal when you know the person's Telegram handle. It works instantly and does not require any country code. This is perfect for messaging businesses, public channels, or anyone who shares their username openly.

**Using a phone number** is useful when you only have someone's phone number and are not sure if they are on Telegram. The t.me/+number format will search for that number on Telegram and open a chat if the user exists. This is great for contacting delivery drivers, service providers, or anyone whose phone number you have but whose Telegram username you do not know.

Both methods are equally private — OPN Chat never stores or transmits your input to any server.

## Is It Safe and Private?

Yes, completely. OPN Chat does not store, log, or track any usernames, phone numbers, or messages you enter. The tool simply generates a `t.me` link and opens it in your browser. All communication happens through Telegram's own secure infrastructure. OPN Chat never intercepts or reads your messages.

Your recently used entries are saved locally in your browser's localStorage for convenience only — so you can quickly re-open a previous chat. You can clear this history at any time by clicking the "Clear" button. Nothing is ever sent to our servers.
