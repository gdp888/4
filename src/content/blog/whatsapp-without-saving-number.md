Every time you need to message someone on WhatsApp — a delivery driver, a landlord, a seller on a marketplace — the default process is the same: save their number, open WhatsApp, find the contact, then start chatting. It works, but it clutters your contacts list with people you may never talk to again.

There is a better way. WhatsApp has a built-in feature called **Click to Chat** that lets you open a conversation with any number without saving it first. OPN Chat makes this feature accessible through a simple, free web interface.

In all these cases, the person does not belong in your phone book. You just need a fast, one-time conversation on WhatsApp.

## How Does It Work?

WhatsApp's Click to Chat uses a special link format: `wa.me/phone_number`. When you open this link in a browser, it launches a WhatsApp conversation with that number — no contact saving required.

OPN Chat simplifies this process. Instead of manually typing URLs, you:

1. **Enter the phone number** with the country code (for example, 7 for Russia, 55 for Brazil, 1 for the USA).
2. **Optionally write a message** that will be pre-filled in the chat.
3. **Click "Open Chat in WhatsApp"** — and you are taken directly to the conversation.

That is it. No sign-up, no app to install, no data stored on any server. Everything runs inside your browser.

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Go to [opnchat.net](https://opnchat.net).
2. In the phone number field, enter the full number **without the + sign**. For example, for a Brazilian number +55 11 99999-9999, type `5511999999999`.
3. If you want to start with a pre-written message, click "Add a message" and type it in.
4. Press the green **"Open Chat in WhatsApp"** button.
5. WhatsApp opens (either the app on your phone or WhatsApp Web on your desktop) with a new conversation ready to go.

## Is It Safe and Private?

Yes. OPN Chat does not store, log, or track any phone numbers or messages you enter. The tool simply generates a WhatsApp link and opens it in your browser. All communication happens through WhatsApp's own secure infrastructure. OPN Chat never intercepts or reads your messages.

Your recently used numbers are saved locally in your browser's localStorage for convenience only — you can clear them at any time by clearing your browser data. Nothing is ever sent to our servers.
